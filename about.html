Who We Are

A youth-led nonprofit organization

Hi! We're STEMina, a youth-led nonprofit organization.

We started this nonprofit because we noticed a disparity in accessible education across the world. The reality is that many children don't receive the same level of education as others. We hope to rewrite that story for countless individuals by building a community that is actively involved in advocacy for accessible education.

A youth-led nonprofit, working toward a world where every child has access to STEM education.

Our Vision

What we're going to do

STEMina offers free education tools for children who want to pursue STEM fields. Through providing online courses, we aim to eliminate geographical barriers and make STEM education more accessible.

As we continue to develop our nonprofit, we will be hosting workshops and will keep you informed regarding future dates!

Our Team

Meet the Founders

Four students who turned a shared frustration with unequal access to STEM education into STEMina.

In truth, I've been incredibly fortunate to have access to the resources and support for education. However, I recognize that is not the reality for many students out there. Not every student is given the same access to resources, guidance, or opportunities because of the circumstances they were born into.

Rather than simply acknowledging this issue, I want to deconstruct the disparity and actively bridge this gap.

To the student who has been denied opportunities... or who also wishes to help change that reality, I will reflect your voice in my work. I will help create a compassionate community where education and opportunities in STEM are not privileges for only a select few, but a possibility for everyone.

Mya WongCo-founder of STEMina

From drawing stick figures to now building digital platforms, education provided me with the skills and understanding of the world that allows me to turn abstract ideas into tangible reality. Having been a receiver for most of my life, I realized my responsibility and passion to become a giver by sharing my learnings with others.

A little more about me — I've always loved being part of innovative and impactful activities, whether through digital creation like gaming, video editing, and animation, or physically through figure skating, drawing, and DIY projects. I'm grateful to have access to opportunities that help me improve every day.

By creating STEMina, I hope STEM education can become accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or where they live. Like our name, STEMina, I hope we can keep our passion alive to learn and grow together.

Hayden LeeCo-founder of STEMina

I used to think learning was just about memorizing facts for tests. But through STEM, I realized it's actually about being curious, asking questions, and never stopping exploring the world around us.

The problem is, not everyone gets the same shot at this kind of education. A lot of students miss out on great opportunities and resources just because of where they live or go to school. That's why we started STEMina.

I hope to help create a welcoming community where students feel encouraged to learn, grow, and explore STEM — whether through educational content, creative projects, and more. I want to inspire others to believe that STEM is something they can be a part of too.

Thivyaa SivalinganathanCo-founder of STEMina

"Growing up, I had access to so many competitions and opportunities that shaped who I am today. Looking back, I hope the privilege I had to access such opportunities can be extended to children who have so much potential of their own.

That's why I believe we should use our advantages to help balance this educational disparity, and why this platform is my way of putting in all the effort I can to make this reality possible!"

Harshita YadavCo-founder of STEMina
